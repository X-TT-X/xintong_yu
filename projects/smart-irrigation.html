2023.12 – 2024.01

Project Code↗All projects ↗

Project overview

The project links environmental sensing, network communication and irrigation control. Sensors collect soil-moisture parameters, and MQTT communication with CVM connects those measurements to automatic sprinkler switching.

My work focused on collecting soil-moisture data and implementing the communication needed to turn the sprinkler on or off automatically. The system illustrates an end-to-end IoT workflow, from sensing conditions in the soil to controlling irrigation equipment.

Key work

  • Used sensors to collect environmental parameters of soil moisture.
  • Communicated with CVM using the MQTT protocol to switch the sprinkler on or off automatically.
