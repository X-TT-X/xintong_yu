Project overview

The presentation examines how robotic systems can contribute to sustainable development through applications in industrial and service settings. It brings together examples of robots performing practical tasks and considers the relationship between automation and sustainability.

The video provides the complete coursework presentation, including its explanations and credited reference footage. It is presented as an academic exploration of robotic applications.
